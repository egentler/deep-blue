The day goes by plain and bland   
I wait until I can rest my hands  
From waiting for an antidote  
Kicking hard  to stay afloat 

All you see is the thin crisp smile   
But I’m begging myself, *don’t put that in the pile*  
There’s far too much—I can’t focus       
Oh look, a chore? That’s a bonus! 

The pile grows and hums a soft note  
As I wait for what this day demotes  
I wake up cold, go to bed with tears   
Hoping tomorrow won’t be full of fears 

Yesterday went, and so will tomorrow   
But fewer days are filled with sorrow  
I’ve found my peace, my joy and fun   
If love is a lottery, I may have won

He’s so sweet and filled with serenity  
And doesn’t treat me like a charity  
So today I will close my case   
This story will continue its chase
