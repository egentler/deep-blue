The world between Life and Death—come one and all it is free, no cost, doesn't matter if you're

white, BIPOC, rich or poor, homeless, homeowner, this is the place for you. It is a place between life

and Death. When you feel you have done all you can and think why do I stay alive—the place to reset

all you have done or all you have been or a place to find the new you. Just bring yourself. You will find

others who share your views, your dreams, your thoughts, your wisdom.  

The great spirit who has your back will still warm your soul and will pat your back to reassure you are

safe and at peace. We ask nothing and all is for the taking.  

A warm breeze blows every day to comfort the soul

The song of the whales as they sing the harmony of the song of the sea

Will surround you with comfort,

The buzz of the bees as they travel around you and harmonize along

Will make you hum along

The whisper of the butterflies as they travel softly through the air

With the spirits from souls long gone

The clatter of the hummingbirds as they chase the scent of flowers

Will comfort you and inspire you

The call of nature as it searches for the soul and inner peace

Of all who come.

There is no clock as time moves by for you to share with someone else or by yourself, 

rest next to the fields of flowers and enjoy the beauty of the vivid colors and the scents. 

Be within yourself to search

and find the answer to the secret question you were born with and share it with all.

Since there is no time nor age you can leave when you want or stay forever

Not all who enter this magical space

Leave with the answer, some have been here a lifetime and still search, with hope

Others find hope and dream and leave with a smile.

No one including you will remember this place but inside something will always,

Be there to let you remember the sounds and the songs.
