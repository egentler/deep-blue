I didn’t know we were borrowing time.    
You didn’t either.    
You had lists and plans and a laugh    
That made the whole room feel possible.    
You talked about all the things you wanted to do in the world   
All the light you were going to bring to it.

I still remember the shape of your voice,  
The voice that used to live in the room with me.    
Kind. Steady.  
A sound that made these hard days feel softer.    
You gave joy away so easily.    
And that laugh got lost in time before we were ready to lose it.  
Before everyone was ready to lose it.

Now I’m stuck listening to you through poor audio.    
Pressed between static and a timestamp.    
Rewind. Play. Rewind again.    
It’s still you, but flattened.    
A memory with background noise.    
Not enough to fill a conversation.    
Just enough to break my heart a little,  
Every time the play button is hit

The cruelest part isn’t just that you’re gone.    
It’s that time kept going without you.    
The world keeps turning.    
Birthdays came and went.    
New songs came out,  
New trends,  
New everything.  
Seasons changed and you weren’t here to see them.    
And I have to watch it all happen    
In a world that feels dimmer without your light in it.

You didn’t know this fate would come.    
You were still becoming.    
Still planning. Still giving.    
And I’m left holding the things you wanted to do,    
and the recordings I play when the quiet gets too loud.

So I keep them.    
Not because they’re enough.    
But because they’re proof.    
Proof that you were here.    
Proof that joy sounded like you.  
Proof that you weren't ever just a dream that never truly happened 

And on the days when grief is heavy,    
I turn the volume up,    
Close my eyes,    
And let your voice,  
Even through the static,   
Remind me what the world felt like    
When you were in it.  
When the world had your light
