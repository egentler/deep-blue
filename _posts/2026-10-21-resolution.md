I am resolved to be 

the silent board you sound upon

when words are verdant balm

and bring a whisper to your lips, 

venting cool to clear the crowding dust 

of stumbling days away. 

To be sustained,

notes held low and laid

like stones well known and numbered,

safe to tread upon when your attention 

must be elsewhere,

full and fierce to stay the storm. 

In desperate doldrums I will be

the living sea 

that bursts itself apart  

to leave you lucent, 

born to fresh belief and movement, 

drops of light to limn your arms. 

Clarion your call, 

I am resolved.
